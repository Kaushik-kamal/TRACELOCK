Public discovery needs a publicly reachable image

Reverse-image search engines fetch a URL — they cannot receive a file. This image only exists on your machine.

TRACELOCK does not silently upload biometric images to third-party services. Your face will never leave this machine unless you explicitly publish it yourself.

Local analysis measures everything possible offline: face detection, quality, and a cryptographic fingerprint. No network request is made and no trust score is produced — scoring requires discovered evidence.
